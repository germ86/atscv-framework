\section{Installation}
Use LuaLaTeX and ensure the class/package files are on your TEXINPUTS path.
\section{Compiler Requirements}
Required tools: LuaLaTeX, pdftotext, qpdf.
\section{Modes}
Supported modes: ats, modern, executive, consulting, compact.
\section{Multilingual Support}
Supported languages: en, de, es, fr, zh, ar, hi using babel + fontspec with safe fallbacks.
\section{ATS-safe Design Rules}
No tabular, multicol, minipage, parbox, sidebars, hidden text, or critical information in headers/footers.
\section{Examples}
Compile and review: examples/fabio-bigtech-modern.tex, examples/fabio-executive.tex, examples/fabio-consulting.tex, examples/fabio-ats.tex.
\section{Validation Workflow}
Compile with LuaLaTeX, extract with pdftotext, and verify with qpdf --check.
